# 📚 Analytics Guide - Understanding Your New Dashboard

## 🎯 Quick Start

Your dashboard now has 8 major analytics sections. Here's what each one tells you about Pamiche's plushie business:

---

## 1. 📈 Growth Metrics (Top Cards)

### What You See
Three cards showing growth percentages with up/down arrows:
- **Mes a Mes** (Month-over-Month)
- **Semana a Semana** (Week-over-Week)  
- **Año a Año** (Year-over-Year)

### What It Means
- **Green ↑**: Sales are growing - good news!
- **Red ↓**: Sales are declining - may need attention
- **Gray −**: No change from previous period

### Example
```
Mes a Mes: +15.3% ($2,340)
Actual: $17,640 | Anterior: $15,300
```
This means you made 15.3% more this month than last month.

### Action Items
- If declining: Check which stores are underperforming
- If growing: Identify what's working and replicate it
- Compare WoW vs MoM to see if growth is accelerating

---

## 2. 🏆 Peak Sales Days

### What You See
Top 5 best revenue days with:
- Date
- Total revenue
- Total profit
- Which stores contributed

### What It Means
These are your "golden days" - understand what made them special:
- Was it a weekend?
- Was it near a holiday?
- Did multiple stores collect on the same day?

### Example
```
🏆 #1: 08/01/2026
Ingresos: $1,010
Ganancia: $660
Tiendas: Delator
```

### Action Items
- Look for patterns (weekends, holidays, events)
- Try to replicate conditions that led to high sales
- Plan collections around similar dates

---

## 3. 📅 Day of Week Performance

### What You See
Bar chart showing total revenue by day (Domingo through Sábado)

### What It Means
- Which days of the week are most profitable
- When people are most likely to play
- Best days to collect money

### Example Insights
- If Saturdays are highest: Weekend foot traffic is key
- If Wednesdays are lowest: Mid-week is slow
- Use this to optimize collection schedule

### Action Items
- Schedule collections on high-revenue days
- Consider restocking before peak days
- Avoid unnecessary trips on slow days

---

## 4. 📊 Moving Averages

### What You See
Line chart with three lines:
- **Gray**: Actual daily revenue (bumpy)
- **Green**: 7-day average (smoothed)
- **Orange**: 30-day average (very smooth)

### What It Means
- Removes daily noise to see real trends
- Green line shows weekly trend
- Orange line shows monthly trend
- If lines are going up: business is growing
- If lines are going down: business is declining

### Example
```
Daily revenue jumps around: $200, $800, $300, $600
But 7-day average shows steady $500/day trend
```

### Action Items
- Focus on trend lines, not daily spikes
- If 7-day crosses above 30-day: acceleration
- If 7-day crosses below 30-day: deceleration

---

## 5. 💰 Cash Flow Timeline

### What You See
Three lines showing cumulative (running total) amounts:
- **Red**: Total money spent on plushies
- **Green**: Total profit earned
- **Orange**: Net (profit minus investment)

### What It Means
- When orange line crosses zero: you've broken even!
- Distance between green and red: your profit margin
- Steeper slopes: faster growth

### Example
```
Start: -$5,000 (initial investment)
Month 3: $0 (break-even!)
Month 6: +$8,000 (profitable!)
```

### Action Items
- Track progress toward break-even
- Identify periods of rapid growth
- Plan reinvestment when cash flow is positive

---

## 6. 🎯 Store Efficiency Ranking

### What You See
Table ranking stores with scores (0-100) based on:
- **Revenue Score** (40%): How much money they make
- **Profit Margin Score** (30%): How profitable they are
- **Consistency Score** (30%): How stable their performance is

### What It Means
- #1 store gets trophy 🏆
- Higher score = better overall performance
- Identifies which stores are doing well and which need help

### Example
```
#1 Time Vault: 92 (high revenue, great margins, consistent)
#2 Delator: 78 (good revenue, okay margins, variable)
#3 XG: 65 (lower revenue, good margins, inconsistent)
```

### Action Items
- Study #1 store: What are they doing right?
- Help low-ranked stores: What can be improved?
- Consider closing stores with consistently low scores

---

## 7. 📊 ROI per Store

### What You See
Table showing for each store:
- Total investment (plushie costs)
- Total revenue
- Total profit
- ROI percentage
- Break-even date

### What It Means
- **ROI > 100%**: You've made back your investment plus profit
- **ROI = 100%**: You've broken even
- **ROI < 100%**: Still recovering investment
- Break-even date: When store became profitable

### Example
```
Delator:
Investment: $4,870
Revenue: $10,590
Profit: $3,456
ROI: 71% (not yet break-even)
Break-even: Pendiente
```

### Action Items
- Prioritize stores with high ROI
- Investigate stores with low ROI
- Set break-even goals for new stores
- Calculate payback period for new locations

---

## 8. 📊 Store Benchmarking

### What You See
Table comparing each store to the average:
- Average daily revenue
- Average daily profit
- Profit margin
- % above/below average
- Status: Top / Normal / Bajo

### What It Means
- **Top** (green): Significantly above average - star performer
- **Normal**: Within expected range
- **Bajo** (red): Significantly below average - needs attention

### Example
```
Time Vault:
Avg Daily Revenue: $542
vs Average: +23.5% ↑ (Top performer)

XG:
Avg Daily Revenue: $387
vs Average: -12.8% ↓ (Below average)
```

### Action Items
- **Top stores**: Document best practices, replicate elsewhere
- **Low stores**: Investigate issues (location, foot traffic, machine condition)
- **Normal stores**: Look for optimization opportunities

---

## 9. 🗓️ Entry Frequency Heatmap

### What You See
Calendar grid with colored squares:
- **Gray**: No collection
- **Light orange**: Low revenue day
- **Dark orange**: High revenue day

### What It Means
- Visual pattern of collection frequency
- Gaps show missed collection opportunities
- Intensity shows revenue per collection

### Example Pattern
```
Week 1: ■ □ □ ■ □ □ ■  (3 collections)
Week 2: ■ ■ □ □ ■ ■ □  (4 collections)
Week 3: □ □ □ ■ □ □ □  (1 collection - gap!)
```

### Action Items
- Identify and fill collection gaps
- Optimize visit frequency per store
- Plan routes to hit multiple stores efficiently
- Avoid over-collecting (too frequent visits)

---

## 💡 How to Use All This Together

### Weekly Review
1. Check **Growth Metrics**: Are we trending up or down?
2. Review **Peak Days**: Any patterns to replicate?
3. Check **Heatmap**: Are we collecting regularly?

### Monthly Review
1. Analyze **Moving Averages**: What's the trend?
2. Review **Store Efficiency**: Which stores are performing?
3. Check **ROI**: Are we on track for profitability?
4. Review **Benchmarks**: Any outliers to address?

### Quarterly Review
1. Analyze **Cash Flow**: When will we break even?
2. Review **YoY Growth**: Long-term trajectory
3. Strategic decisions: Open new stores? Close underperformers?

## 🚀 Pro Tips

1. **Use Date Range Filter**: Compare different time periods
2. **Look for Patterns**: Holidays, weekends, seasons
3. **Focus on Trends**: Not individual days
4. **Compare Stores**: Learn from top performers
5. **Track Progress**: Set goals and measure against them
6. **Be Consistent**: Regular collections = better data
7. **Act on Insights**: Data is only useful if you use it!
